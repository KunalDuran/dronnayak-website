Why Dronnayak

Drone teams keep rebuilding the same plumbing.

Fragile connectivity

Cellular links drop, NAT breaks things, and telemetry pipelines built in-house rarely survive contact with the field.

Limited observability

Lack of real-time insights increases operational risk.

Complex Integration

Fragmented tools make scaling multi-vendor fleets difficult.

What the platform does

Connectivity as infrastructure, not a side project.

Real-time telemetry

Stream MAVLink from Pixhawk to the cloud over reliable, reconnecting channels.

Fleet management

A unified dashboard for multi-drone operations, with session and access control.

Edge deployment

Push containers or binaries to companion computers in the field, with rollback.

Security

Encrypted transport and role-based access for operators, developers, and viewers.
